# GT Termin — App Store & Google Play listing

Draft texts to paste into App Store Connect and Google Play Console.
Each limit is noted next to the field, and every text below fits it (checked by script).
Prices and subscription plans are left out on purpose: Apple rejects store text that steers users to purchases made outside the app.

**Languages per store:** Google Play supports Macedonian (mk-MK), English and Albanian (sq), so all three sections can be used there.
App Store Connect has no Macedonian or Albanian localization, so on the App Store use the English section. If you want Macedonian text to show to App Store users in Macedonia, paste it into an English locale such as English (U.K.).

## Screenshots: how to take them

An iPhone 16 Pro Max screenshot (side button + volume up) is exactly 1320×2868, the size App Store Connect asks for under 6.9" Display. No resizing is needed.

1. Use a business account with realistic but non-private data. Customer names and phone numbers in the shot are visible to everyone in the store, so use a test business or blur them.
2. Pick one theme for all shots (the default light glass theme reads best in the store) and one language per set.
3. Suggested 5 screens, in this order:
   1. Business home: today's bookings and revenue at a glance
   2. Calendar: a full day with several team members
   3. Customer booking flow: choosing a time slot
   4. Clients: customer list or a customer's history
   5. Analytics / reports
4. App Store needs 3–10 screenshots; Google Play needs 2–8.
